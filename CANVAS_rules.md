What is different to other handwritten notetaking apps is speed. The premis of autocomplete is speed of notetaking when in lecture. 

It will be expected that the app will have to deal with notes having hundreds of pages, each page having hundreds of strokes. How we deal this scale needs to be efficent, simple, fast.

In the app:
- Canvas will be apple pencil draws only
- 1 finger panning
- 2 finger zooming in & out via pinching
- Importing pdfs will result in them being backgrounds for the page (with pdf page size setting the canvas size)
-  Strokes will be vectorized as well

How the canvas connects to prototype/
- The models in prototype/autocomplete/models will be used to read what the user is writing.
